## 2024-05-18 - Proper Form Label Association in React
**Learning:** In React components, input elements mapped dynamically (like from a list) often lack proper label association if their IDs are not explicitly linked via `htmlFor` on the corresponding `<label>`. Relying purely on visual layout leads to poor screen reader accessibility. Also, standard focus states (`focus-visible:ring-2`) and specific ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label` for icons) are often omitted during rapid prototyping but are essential for basic keyboard accessibility and screen readers.
**Action:** Always ensure dynamic inputs generate unique IDs and pair them with explicit `htmlFor` attributes on their labels. Verify that interactive toggle buttons carry `aria-expanded` and that icon-only/date-picker inputs have a descriptive `aria-label`.
