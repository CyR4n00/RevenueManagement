## 2024-06-13 - Modal Accessibility Enhancements
**Learning:** Adding native modal behaviors (Escape key to dismiss and backdrop click to dismiss) significantly improves the accessibility and usability of custom modal implementations. In combination with the appropriate `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby` tags, it ensures that screen readers accurately announce the modal to users, providing a complete keyboard-accessible UX.
**Action:** When implementing custom modals in React, always include: a `useEffect` for the 'Escape' key event listener attached to the window, an `onClick` on the outer backdrop container mapped to the close function, `e.stopPropagation()` on the inner modal content container to prevent accidental closures, and the required ARIA modal tags (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby`).
