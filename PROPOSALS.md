# Revenue Assistant - Future Proposals

This document outlines proposed features and enhancements for future iterations of the Revenue Assistant tool, moving beyond the initial MVP.

## 1. Advanced Competitor Benchmarking
- **Room Type & Meal Plan Filtering:** Evolve from simply tracking the Best Available Rate (BAR) to allowing users to filter and benchmark specific room categories (e.g., Suite vs. Standard) and inclusions (e.g., Breakfast included vs. Room Only).

## 2. Enhanced Data Visualization
- **Price History Popups:** Introduce interactive UI elements where clicking on a competitor's price in the Revenue Tower opens a popup chart showing the historical price trends over a longer period (e.g., past 30-90 days).

## 3. Reporting & Analytics
- **PDF and CSV Export Expansion:** Build upon the current PMS CSV export feature to include automated, formatted PDF reports suitable for executive summaries and internal revenue meetings.
- **Revenue Impact Calculation:** Introduce predictive analytics to estimate the potential impact on overall revenue (RevPAR) based on the AI's suggested pricing actions versus inaction.
