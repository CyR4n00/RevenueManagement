# Revenue Assistant Future Improvement Proposals

This document tracks potential features and enhancements for future iterations of the Revenue Assistant tool.

## Planned Improvements

*   **Room Type & Plan Filtering:** Currently, the system tracks the Best Available Rate (BAR). Future versions should allow users to filter competitor pricing by specific room types (e.g., Single, Twin, Suite) and plan conditions (e.g., Breakfast included, Non-refundable).
*   **Price History Popups:** Implement interactive charts or popups in the Revenue Calendar to view the historical price trend for a specific date and competitor over the past 30-90 days.
*   **Enhanced PDF/CSV Exports:** Expand the reporting capabilities to generate comprehensive PDF reports for management meetings and advanced CSV exports that integrate seamlessly with various PMS systems.
*   **Revenue Impact Calculations:** Introduce a module to estimate the potential revenue impact (uplift or loss) of adopting the AI's suggested pricing based on historical booking curves and estimated market demand.
*   **Direct PMS/OTA Integration:** Move from CSV exports to direct, two-way API integrations with popular Property Management Systems and site controllers to automatically apply approved price recommendations.
